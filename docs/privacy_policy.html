Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

PDF Scanner ("the App") is developed by Son Le ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Camera Access

The App requires camera permission to scan documents. Images captured by the camera are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your scanned images or generated PDF files on any external server.

1.2 Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase services to improve the App:

  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymized usage data such as screen views, feature usage frequency, and app events. No personal content (document text, images) is collected.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports including device model, OS version, and stack traces to help us fix bugs.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — measures app performance metrics (screen load times, network latency).

These services may collect device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

1.3 Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your consent preferences. You can manage ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

We use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent for personalized advertising where required by law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

1.4 Local Storage

Scanned documents (PDF files) and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's internal storage and a local SQLite database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share or export it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal contact information.
  • We do not access, read, or transmit the content of your scanned documents.
  • We do not require user accounts or login credentials.
  • We do not use cloud storage for your files (all processing is on-device).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide core scanning functionality (camera → crop → filter → PDF).
  • To improve app stability and performance through crash and analytics reports.
  • To display ads to support free access to the App.
  • To manage feature flags and app configuration via Firebase Remote Config.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Anonymized analytics and crash data is processed by Google Firebase under their data processing terms.

5. Data Retention

Scanned documents remain on your device until you delete them through the App or uninstall the App. Firebase analytics and crash data is retained according to Google's retention policies (typically 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics).

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising via your device's Google ad settings.
  • Delete all locally stored data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.
  • Request deletion of analytics data by contacting us.

8. Security

All document processing happens on-device. We use industry-standard practices to protect the limited data we collect through Firebase services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
